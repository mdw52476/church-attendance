Privacy Policy

Forty Corners Church attendance app · Last updated November 24, 2026

What this app is

Church Attendance is a private tool used by our church's volunteers and staff to record who attends services and events, and to follow up with guests. It is not a public service.

What information is stored

  • Names (or a short description when a name isn't known), age group, usual seating section, and family groupings.
  • Optional contact details a volunteer chooses to enter, such as phone, email, address, birthdate and notes.
  • Which events each person attended, and which volunteer recorded or changed the attendance, and when.

Where it is stored

All attendance information is stored in a Google Sheet owned by the church. The app has no database of its own. Only people the church has shared that Google Sheet with can view or change the information.

Signing in

Volunteers sign in with their name and a church passcode. The app does not use or see anyone's personal Google account. The name a volunteer enters is recorded with the changes they make, so the church can see who took attendance.

To keep volunteers signed in, the app stores an encrypted sign-in cookie on the volunteer's own device. It contains only their name. Signing out in the app's Settings removes it, and changing the church passcode signs everyone out.

The app reaches the church's Google Sheet through a Google service account that belongs to the church. It can only open sheets the church has shared with it.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone outside the church.
  • We do not use it for advertising, and the app contains no ads or tracking.

Corrections and removal

If you would like to see, correct or remove the information the church has recorded about you or your family, please contact the church office and we will take care of it.
